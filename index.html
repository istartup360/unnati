About Us

Who We Are

Unnati Charitable Trust (meaning "Growth") was born from the belief that every person has the power to create positive change. We are a youth-driven NGO , J&K.

Our volunteers come from all walks of life — students, professionals, homemakers — united by one mission: to serve those who need it most. We work at the grassroots level.

From busy food drives to quiet school supply distributions, every effort is driven by empathy and community trust built over the years.
